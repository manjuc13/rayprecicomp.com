A Focused Partner for Automotive Tube Assemblies

Ray Precicomp is a specialist automotive component company focused on polymer tube assemblies and related sub-assemblies. We aim to support Tier-1 customers with responsive engineering coordination, practical manufacturing solutions and a quality-focused approach.

Our Focus

We concentrate on tube-based routing applications where material selection, formed geometry, connector integration, packaging and repeatable assembly are important to product performance.

How We Work

  • Understand the customer’s application, drawing and specifications.
  • Review routing, packaging and manufacturability considerations.
  • Support prototype development and fitment feedback.
  • Coordinate component processing and assembly requirements.
  • Support inspection, validation activities and production readiness.

Our Vision

To become a trusted partner for automotive tube assemblies through engineering collaboration, quality, continuous improvement and customer value.

Our Partnership Approach

We work as a component and sub-assembly partner to automotive Tier-1 suppliers. Our objective is to complement customers’ system expertise and support their product and manufacturing requirements.
