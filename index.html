Advanced Plastic Pipe Assemblies for Automotive Applications

Lightweight, leak-tight and application-engineered plastic pipe assemblies for fluid, air, coolant and urea routing across ICE, HEV, BEV and energy-storage platforms.

Company Overview

Ray Precicomp develops and supplies application-specific plastic pipe assemblies for automotive and mobility systems. Our solutions are designed to meet functional, packaging and durability requirements of modern vehicles, including BS-VI emission systems and electric-vehicle thermal architectures.

We collaborate closely with OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers to convert system requirements into ready-to-install routing solutions, integrating pipes, connectors, protection elements and auxiliary components as defined by the vehicle architecture.

Our focus is on delivering consistent geometry, reliable sealing performance and long-term durability under demanding operating conditions involving pressure, temperature variation, vibration and aggressive media.

Core Capabilities

  • Complex 3D routing for tight installation spaces
  • Dimensional repeatability across production volumes
  • Supply as individual pipes or complete routed sub-assemblies
  • Media compatibility for fuel, urea, coolant, air and hydrogen
  • Coverage across passenger cars, CVs, off-highway and energy storage
  • OEM-aligned quality systems suitable for PPAP and series production

Product & Application Portfolio

Plastic pipe assemblies for fuel feed, return and vent circuits, engineered to maintain sealing integrity and dimensional stability throughout vehicle life.

  • Media: Diesel, biodiesel, gasoline, ethanol blends (E10–E100)
  • Tube construction: Monolayer PA tubes, multilayer PA/EVOH tubes, hose-pipe combinations
  • Routing: Straight, curved and free-form geometries
  • Connections: Automotive quick-connect systems as per customer standards
  • Applications: Passenger cars, commercial vehicles, off-road platforms

Customized urea delivery systems designed to ensure reliable dosing under cold-start, frost and high-temperature operating conditions.

  • Unheated, electrically heated and coolant-heated configurations
  • Plain, bent and complex free-form pipe routings
  • Integration of manifolds and multi-connector interfaces
  • Support for compact packaging and multi-injection layouts

Coolant routing solutions for engines, electric-vehicle batteries, power electronics and energy-storage systems.

  • Monolayer and multilayer tube constructions
  • Straight, curved and corrugated geometries
  • Integration of valves, temperature sensors and pressure sensors
  • Designed for thermal stability and vibration resistance

Plastic pipe assemblies for compressed-air routing in braking and auxiliary pneumatic systems.

  • Nylon tubes, hose combinations and hybrid assemblies
  • Secure connection concepts for vibration-prone environments
  • Integration of valves, test points and auxiliary components

Complete air-suspension routing solutions for passenger cars and light commercial vehicles.

  • Curved and straight pipe geometries
  • Integration of expansion volumes and valve blocks
  • Protection against abrasion, heat and environmental exposure

Routing solutions for hydrogen systems developed with a focus on tightness, safety and long-term material stability.

  • Multilayer tube constructions with dedicated barrier layers
  • Low and medium pressure applications (up to 21 bar)
  • Operating temperature range: –40 °C to +90 °C
  • Designed for minimal permeation and secure connections
