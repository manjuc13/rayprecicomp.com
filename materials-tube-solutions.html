Material and Tube Construction Options

The right tube material and construction depend on the application’s fluid, operating conditions, routing geometry, permeability targets and customer specifications. Ray Precicomp supports material and construction discussions as part of component development.

PA11

May be considered for applications requiring flexibility and chemical resistance, subject to the selected grade and application specification.

PA12

May be considered for selected fuel, SCR/urea and pneumatic routing applications where its properties meet the defined requirements.

PA6 / PA66

May be considered for selected pneumatic, air-suspension, HVAC or coolant-return applications where the material grade is suitable.

Multilayer Barrier Tubes

Multilayer constructions, including polymer/barrier/polymer combinations, may be required where permeability or fuel-barrier performance is a design priority.

Material Selection Checklist

  • Fluid and chemical compatibility
  • Operating and transient temperature
  • Pressure or vacuum requirements
  • Permeability and emissions requirements
  • Tube dimensions, bend radius and package constraints
  • Connector compatibility and validation plan

Important Note

This page describes general material families, not a blanket approval for any specific use. The exact grade, dimensions and application suitability must be confirmed against customer specifications and supplier data.
