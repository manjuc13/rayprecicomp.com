Engineering Support for Component Development

Ray Precicomp collaborates with customer engineering and purchasing teams to support component-level tube assembly development. Our focus is on understanding the drawing, application constraints and manufacturing requirements early in the development process.

Application and Drawing Review

Review available drawings, application information, interfaces and packaging constraints to identify points that may require clarification or development.

Routing and Forming Feasibility

Discuss bend geometry, straight lengths, access and routing considerations against the proposed tube assembly and package.

Material and Construction Review

Support discussions on tube material and mono-layer or multilayer construction options based on the application specification.

Connector and Component Integration

Review interfaces, assembly sequence and integration requirements for the connectors and components specified by the customer.

Prototype and Fitment Feedback

Support prototype build and fitment feedback so that issues can be identified and addressed during development.

Production Readiness

Coordinate component processing, assembly, inspection and customer-defined validation activities in preparation for serial supply.

Discuss your application

Share your drawing, application details or requirements with Ray Precicomp.
