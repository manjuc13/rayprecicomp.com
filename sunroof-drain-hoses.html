Sunroof Drain Hoses

Sunroof drain hoses route water from the roof drainage channel to designated vehicle outlets. The hose path must fit within the body package and support the drainage and environmental requirements defined for the vehicle.

Application Function

The hose carries water away from the sunroof drainage channel and guides it through the vehicle body to the specified outlet. Routing and connection details depend on the vehicle layout.

Design Considerations

  • Routing through confined body spaces
  • Flexibility for installation and package variation
  • Kink resistance and maintaining the required drainage path
  • Connection and retention at the drainage channel and outlet
  • Exposure to weather, moisture and the vehicle environment
  • Fitment and assembly sequence

Hose and Interface Selection

Hose material, dimensions, connector details and retention features should be selected according to the customer’s drawing and environmental requirements. The selected design should maintain the intended drainage path within the available package.
