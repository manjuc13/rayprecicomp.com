Manufacturing and Quality Approach

Ray Precicomp focuses on consistent component processing and assembly aligned with customer drawings, specifications and quality requirements. Inspection and validation activities are defined according to the part and application.

Manufacturing Process

  • Review of tube specification and construction
  • Tube cutting and end preparation
  • Tube forming and routing
  • Connector and component assembly
  • Inspection and application-specific testing
  • Packing and supply according to agreed requirements

Quality Planning

Quality planning should be aligned with the customer’s drawing, control plan, approval requirements and agreed inspection criteria.

Inspection and Testing

Inspection methods and tests are selected for the specific part and customer requirements. Do not assume every test applies to every product; test methods and acceptance criteria must be agreed for each application.

Customer Collaboration

Ray Precicomp works with customers to address drawing clarifications, fitment feedback, documentation and production-quality requirements.
